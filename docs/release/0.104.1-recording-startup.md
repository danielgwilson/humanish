# 0.104.1 — Recording startup

Mixed desktop recording no longer waits behind the recording mix's default
two-second PulseAudio buffer. Its null sink now uses the supported no-rewind mode,
which bounds that buffer to 50 ms. FFmpeg settings, timestamps, startup ordering
and resource ownership are unchanged; screen-only recording uses the same path
as before.

The reproduced Linux VM baseline had a 1.67-second gap after its first frame.
The corrected recording began video at 0.187 seconds and audio at 0.244 seconds,
with continuous frame cadence. Six browser flashes and tones were retained and
aligned within one 15 fps video frame. Actual E2B mixed and screen-only captures
also had continuous video cadence. These are retained checks, not universal
startup-latency guarantees or a new Mac acceptance study. Longer installed checks
also retained variable guest capture gaps, including a 1.33-second mid-recording
stall. Those gaps remain tracked in #854; this correction addresses the cold mix
buffer.

Device and transport startup still precede capture. Cold audio sources may deliver
sound later than the playback request; early silent audio is not proof that every
requested sound was captured. Original recordings are preserved, with no fabricated
frames or timestamp repair. Interrupted recording still produces partial metadata
and releases its recorder and audio processes. Screenshots remain the default
and the fallback if optional recording fails.
