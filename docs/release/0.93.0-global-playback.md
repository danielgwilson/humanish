# Humanish 0.93.0

The Observer uses one study clock across the participant grid and individual
recordings. Seek halfway through a study, open a participant, then scrub back:
returning to the grid shows every participant at that same time. Playback also
continues through ordinary participant navigation and browser Back/Forward.

A compact bottom row holds play/pause, study time and the scrubber. It stays
available while scrolling and reserves space for evidence. Playback options
contains speed and **Follow live** or **Latest captures**. The sidebar and shell
keep their layout across views, including phone widths.

Study time uses recorded capture timestamps. Before a participant's first
capture, the viewer shows that no capture is available yet; after their final
capture, it holds that image with its age. Missing or invalid timestamps keep
that participant's local recording controls, pausing the study clock until the
reviewer returns. Findings and comparison retain their local evidence review.

Opening an explicit frame, event or saved moment pauses and seeks to its
associated capture. Exact frame identity is preserved even when two captures
have the same timestamp. Ordinary participant navigation preserves the clock
instead of repeatedly seeking to the displayed capture.

This works in self-contained HTML exports and existing recorded bundles. It
does not start participants, analysis or provider requests. Sparse captures
still cannot show what happened between recorded images.
