## What the runs found about the harness, not the app

The first attempt at this run, six lanes started 20 s apart at 18:44 UTC, lost **five of six before
any participant turn** to the E2B SDK: `12: [unimplemented] HTTP 404` on four (the desktop SDK's
first request into a sandbox whose envd was not routable yet, thrown from inside `Sandbox.create`
before the run learned an id), `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'envdVersion')` on one
(a create reply without a body) and `Expected to receive information about written file` on one
(the archive upload). A probe of the same installed SDK one minute later created a sandbox, wrote
7 MB and killed it in 6 s; status.e2b.dev carries two "elevated error rates and slower sandbox start
times" incidents this week. The one lane that ran to a session was the sixth.

Two of the four `HTTP 404` sandboxes were still running afterwards and were killed by hand by id;
one of those lanes' CLI processes also stayed alive twelve minutes past its written result holding
one socket to `api.e2b.dev` (#581, second data point). #630 retries a create or upload once on
those shapes. This run, 40 s apart from the #630 branch, needed no retry: 0 of 6 lanes recorded
one. Run with `HUMANISH_DEBUG_HANDLES=1`: five lanes settled with no active resource, two with one
`TCPSocketWrap` that closed within a minute.
